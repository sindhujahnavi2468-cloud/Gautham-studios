Three Decades
and Still Counting

For over three decades, Gautam Studio & Films has stood as a trusted name in photography and filmmaking — capturing weddings, portraits, and life's most precious occasions with warmth, patience, and an eye trained by years of experience.

Founded in 1990 by Gautam, the studio has grown from a single camera and a passion for storytelling into a full creative practice — blending old-world craftsmanship with a distinctly modern, cinematic sensibility in every frame.

The Studio Behind the Legacy

Since 1990, Gautam Studio & Films has been documenting weddings, portraits, and family milestones with a steady hand and a genuine love for the craft. Over the decades, the studio has evolved with every new generation of couples it has served.

What hasn't changed is the philosophy: every photograph should feel true. The glance between two people, the laughter mid-conversation, the quiet moment before a ceremony begins — these are the frames GS Films chases, year after year.
